How it works

Three taps to your daily reading

No sign up. No complicated setup. Open Mewacle in your browser or on your phone and let the cats do their thing.

01

Choose your focus

Type a question if you like, then pick the area of your life you want guidance on: love, career, money, family, health or school.

02

Draw your card

Hold your question in mind and tap to reveal. Cards can appear upright or reversed for deeper nuance.

03

Read your guidance

Get a reading written for your chosen focus area, a quick yes/no take, and a cat wisdom quote to carry with you.

Everything inside

More than a card a day

Mewacle grows with you. Here is what is waiting inside the app.

🔮

Daily oracle reading

Draw from a 36-card deck, upright or reversed, with a reading written for the focus area you chose. Not happy with the pull? Redraw.

🌙

Daily horoscope

Pick your sign for today's horoscope, built from real astronomical positions and original writing. Tap Deep dive for a take on each of the six life areas, plus a note from your sign's cat.

⚡

Quick Take

Asked a yes-or-no question? Every reading comes with a quick lean, from a clear yes to a gentle no, based on the card you drew and which way it landed.

📜

Card history and collection

Every draw is saved with the question you asked, so you can look back by month. Watch your collection fill in as you uncover more of the deck.

✨

Rare card pulls

Every so often a special version of a card slips into your draw. When it does, you get a full reveal. Keep drawing to find them.

💌

Share your readings

Send your card pull or today's horoscope to a friend with one tap.

🐱

Six cats, one voice each

Meet the clowder inside the app, and see which cat looks after your zodiac sign. Add your name in Settings and they will greet you by it.

📱

Use it anywhere

Open it in any browser, add it to your home screen for one-tap access, or get the Android app. On Android you can also turn on a gentle daily reminder.

🃏 New cards join the deck regularly. Coming next: multi-card spreads, so the cats can tell the whole story.
